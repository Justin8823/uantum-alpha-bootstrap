## The Problem

Most AI trading tools fail because:
- **Single AI = single point of failure** — one wrong call wipes you out
- **Opaque fund custody** — you don't know where your money is
- **Static algorithms** — they stop learning after deployment

## The Solution: AI Committee Voting

Quantum Alpha uses **3 AI models** that vote on every trade. No consensus = no trade.

```
Claude (Analysis) + DeepSeek (Vision) + Kimi (Risk) 
        → Majority Vote → Execute or Reject
        → Learn from outcome → Update weights
```

Every decision is **logged on-chain**. Immutable. Verifiable.
